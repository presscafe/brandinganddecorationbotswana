Privacy notice

This is a prototype notice for a pre-revenue business. It must be reviewed by a Botswana-qualified advisor before you collect personal data from customers. Nothing in this repository is legal advice.

1. What we collect — and what we don't (yet)

The quote engine on the home page runs entirely in your browser. It sends nothing to our servers, stores nothing remotely, and sets no tracking cookies. In this prototype state, we do not collect names, emails, or phone numbers through the site.

When you contact us via WhatsApp or email, we receive only what you choose to send (e.g. your name, organisation, quantity, artwork). We use it solely to prepare your quote, deliver your order, and keep a record for warranty and tax purposes.

2. Why we process personal data (when we do)

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a quotation — at your request.
  • To perform a contract — to produce, brand, and deliver goods you order.
  • To comply with the law — tax invoices (BURS), company records (CIPA), and lawful requests.
  • With your consent — e.g. adding you to a WhatsApp broadcast list or requesting a reference for a case study. You can withdraw consent at any time.

3. Botswana Data Protection Act 2024

Botswana's Data Protection Act No. 18 of 2024 is in force. Where it applies to us:

  • We collect the minimum personal information needed for the purpose.
  • We keep a Botswana-resident copy of personal data and restrict access to authorised staff only.
  • We encrypt backups and limit retention (see §6).
  • We will notify the Information and Data Protection Commission (IDPC) within 72 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach where required, and affected individuals where there is significant risk.
  • A Data Protection Officer is appointed before large-scale processing begins. In the interim the Director is the responsible person.

Verify registration obligations and cross-border transfer rules directly with the IDPC (idpc.org.bw) and with counsel.

4. What we never do

  • No sale of personal data.
  • No counterfeit or untraceable safety equipment — traceability is a procurement rule, stated in our README.
  • No personal data in public task trackers (Linear/Notion/GitHub). Task trackers hold pointers to files in Google Drive, where access is controlled.
  • No sharing of client artwork or employee size matrices outside the fulfilment chain without written permission.

5. Sharing

We share personal data only with: (a) vetted production/delivery partners strictly to fulfil your order; (b) professional advisors (auditor, clearing agent, lawyer) under confidentiality; (c) where the law requires it. Partners operate under a written subcontract and QC annexure that includes confidentiality obligations.

6. Retention

  • Quotation records: 12 months.
  • Order, delivery, and tax records: 7 years (tax/company law).
  • Artwork/Brand Vault files: for the duration of the client relationship, deleted within 90 days of termination on request.
  • Enquiry messages that do not become orders: 12 months, then deleted.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention duties), object to processing, or withdraw consent for direct marketing. Contact us using the details on the home page and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Contact

Data controller: Branding & Decoration Botswana, Gaborone, Botswana.
Email: to be published when a monitored inbox is live (prototype: the address in store/assets/app.js is a placeholder).
Postal: to be added with the trade licence address.

9. Updates

We update this notice when the site begins to store enquiries, run accounts, or use analytics. The updated version will carry a new date at the top.
