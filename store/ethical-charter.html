Ethical Charter

We win procurement on transparency, not tricks. This page is the plain-language version of docs/ethical-charter.md — secular, audit-ready, and compatible with both ESG and Shariah-aware review. No religious language is used with clients unless they request it.

1. How we charge — no hidden interest

Cash price is cost + disclosed margin (28–35% depending on quantity). If you need 30 days to pay, the quote shows a fixed +2% line item — not compounding daily interest, not a penalty. You choose before ordering: cash or 30d at the fixed uplift. No hidden fees. That is a cost-plus markup you can audit, not interest on a loan.

2. No ambiguity — the spec is the contract

Every quote states garment spec, decoration method (embroidery stitches / screen colours / DTF), placement, size matrix, quantity, unit price, VAT 14%, delivery fee/zone, lead time, and validity (30 days stocked / 14 days import). When we say 72h, we mean a stocked SA-trade blank finished in Gaborone — never an import that needs BOBS CoC.

3. Provenance — we show the certificate

Each SKU has its test report or Certificate of Conformity per BOBS CoC. We attach it to a quote in <4 minutes (BDB-022) or we tell you we cannot supply. Leather is flagged by source (bovine/PU — never porcine without explicit consent) so halal-aware buyers can decide.

4. Fair partners, fair workers

Partners are month-to-month on a scope sheet, scored on quality/on-time/communication, 3 strikes before termination (BDB-017). They are paid ≤7 days. Two operators per machine remove single-person risk. Rework ≤2% at our cost if our defect (SOP-09).

5. Data as trust (amanah)

Your logo, size matrix and price list are trust. Minimal PII, encrypted, with a Botswana-resident copy weekly and 72-hour breach notice (BDB-030 + privacy.html). Partners see only their job and must delete it within 30 days.

6. Community — 2.5% back

2.5% of annual net profit funds school uniforms or fit-for-purpose workwear for under-resourced learners — tracked at month-end, disbursed locally. Not marketed; reported.
