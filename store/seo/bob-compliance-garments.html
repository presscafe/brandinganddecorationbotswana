BOBS CoC for garments — what it really means

Botswana Bureau of Standards compulsory standards: garments are on the compulsory list. Imported garments must be inspected at country of origin and hold a Certificate of Conformity before arrival. No CoC = held at border.

Since 1 Apr 2024

Regulations require regulated products to obtain CoC before arrival. Budget 2–6 weeks + inspection cost. SA-trade stock (Amrod BW) is already cleared; direct China/Jasani import is where CoC bites. We budget time/cost in every landed calc (see data/suppliers.json → channel: import).

How we pass where micro shops fail

We pre-classify HS (clearing agent), book inspection at origin, file CoC per shipment, keep certificate per SKU in admin.html → Certificates — attachable in <4 min (BDB-022). That is tender-grade compliance, not marketing.
