Terms of indicative quotations

Prototype terms. Reviewed against docs/06-compliance-risk-register.md and docs/04-commercial-model-unit-economics.md. Not legal advice — have a Botswana lawyer review before use with paying clients.

1. Indicative, not binding

Every price the quote engine shows is indicative, generated in your browser from modelled costs. It is not an offer, not a contract, and not a commitment to supply at that price. A firm quotation follows verified supplier pricing, artwork review, stock confirmation, and — for first orders — a deposit (see §4).

2. Validity

  • Stocked lines: 30 days.
  • Imported lines / lines with SACU duty: 14 days (FX and duty move).
  • After expiry a new firm quote is required.

3. Price structure

Unless the firm quote says otherwise, prices are: ex-works Gaborone plus the delivery line shown; exclusive of VAT until VAT is added (currently 14%); and exclusive of design beyond one proof round. Volume tiers, decoration setup charges, delivery thresholds, credit surcharges (+2% per 30 days), and rush surcharges (+20% for ≤72 hours) are as stated in the quote panel and store/data/catalogue.json. SACU duty (clothing 40–45% MFN on extra-union imports) is embedded in landed costs per channel — verify per HS code with a clearing agent before ordering imported garments.

4. Payment

  • First order from a new account: 50% deposit before production, balance on collection/delivery.
  • Repeat accounts: 30-day terms only with an approved credit limit. 60-day terms only for blue-chip accounts backed by a facility. Government work only with a facility in place.
  • Late payment may incur interest and suspension of further supply.
  • Cash conversion cycle is monitored: if receivables exceed policy, further credit sales pause.

5. Artwork, Brand Vault and approvals

You warrant that you own or are licensed to use any logo/artwork you supply. We digitise for embroidery at the setup charge shown; colour and size are confirmed via first-article approval (one finished unit photographed/signed before the bulk run on orders >100 units). Approval is written. Client-approved artwork errors are chargeable for rework; our defects are remade or credited within 5 working days.

6. Quality and compliance

  • No counterfeit, grey-market or untraceable safety equipment is ever supplied. Certificates/test reports are kept per SKU and attachable to any quote within 4 minutes.
  • Garment imports subject to Botswana Bureau of Standards (BOBS) compulsory standards require a Certificate of Conformity (CoC) before arrival — allow 2–6 weeks.
  • PPE is supplied against the specification you approve. Where you specify EN/ISO standards (e.g. EN ISO 20471 hi-vis, EN 361, EN 345), we supply the manufacturer's test report for the specific SKU — marketing claims are not evidence.
  • Defect/rework target: ≤2%. Wash, cure and placement are checked against a written QC checklist per decoration method.

7. Delivery

Lead times are working days from deposit + approved artwork + stock confirmation. Delivery fees per zone are shown in the quote engine; orders over P25,000 normally ship free. Remote mine-gate drops are priced per drop. Proof of delivery is photographed and signed; risk passes on delivery/collection.

8. Returns and rework

Custom branded goods are made to order and cannot be returned for change of mind. Where we are at fault (wrong size mix per approved matrix, misplacement, colour out-of-tolerance, defective blank) we remake or credit within 5 working days after investigation. Log every defect — root cause is recorded.

9. Ethics

No improper inducements, no bid manipulation, no informal commissions to procurement staff, no fabrication of local-content, citizen-ownership or certificate claims. Misrepresentation in PPRA tenders carries disqualification and prosecution. We state this publicly because compliance-conscious buyers value it.

10. Liability

To the extent permitted by Botswana law, liability for any single order is limited to the price of that order. Consequential loss is excluded. Nothing limits liability for fraud or where the law forbids limitation. We maintain public liability, goods-in-transit, and — before large safety-critical contracts — product liability cover.

11. Governing law

Botswana law. Disputes are resolved by negotiation in the first instance, then by the courts of Botswana sitting in Gaborone.
