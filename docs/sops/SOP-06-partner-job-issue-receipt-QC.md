# SOP-06 — Partner job issue, in-progress check and receipt QC

**Purpose:** partner work never reaches the client without our eyes on it. The promise to the client is ours — the defect cost is the partner's to prevent.

**Trigger:** any job routed to a network partner (overflow, out-of-town, specialist).

**Owner:** Production lead. **Revision:** 2026-10-29 v1.

---

## 1. Issue

1. Written scope only — WhatsApp is for coordination, not for scope. Scope sheet includes:
   - Product + quantity + size breakdown (exact)
   - Logo version + placement diagram + thread/Pantone refs (from Brand Vault)
   - Decoration method, stitch count / colours / positions
   - Deadline (date + time), delivery address, packing instruction
   - Price, payment terms (on our payment to partner: 30 days from client receipt), defect liability
2. Partner confirms in writing: “Accepted — {quantity} × {product} by {deadline} at {price}.”
3. Attach QC annexure (signed) — partner has it on file after onboarding (BDB-017). No annexure = no job.

## 2. In-progress check

- For jobs >100 units or >P10,000: request a **photo of the first finished unit** before bulk (same standard as SOP-03). Approve in writing.
- For tight deadlines: a mid-run count update at 50%.
- Any deviation (shortage, colour, placement) is a written change order — never verbal.

## 3. Receipt QC (100% for first 3 jobs with a new partner; thereafter sampling)

On arrival in Gaborone, before any client-facing packing:

- [ ] Count vs scope sheet (by size). Shortage = partner's to make good at their cost + freight.
- [ ] Random sample: 1 in 10 (min 5 units) checked for placement (±3 mm), colour, stitch/print quality, backing/trim, size labels.
- [ ] Wash/cure spot-check where relevant (screen: cure test — stretch and tape; DTF: 24h adhesion check).
- [ ] Photos: 3 units (front + detail) for the job pack.

**Pass:** proceed to kitting (SOP-07). **Fail:** quarantine the batch, notify partner in writing with photos within 24 hours, request rework plan and revised delivery date. Log defect.

## 4. Scorecard entry

After every job, update the partner scorecard (sheet):

| Field | How scored |
|---|---|
| On-time % | Delivered by deadline? (Yes/No; late by hours) |
| Defect % | Defective units ÷ total |
| Communication | Responded within 4 hours, proactively flagged risks? |
| Price adherence | Matched quoted price? |
| Capacity honesty | Accepted only what could be delivered? |

Monthly review: top 2 partners get priority volume; bottom partner gets a written improvement plan; 3 strikes in 6 months = removal.

## 5. Payment

- Invoicing handled by us to the client. Partner invoiced to us.
- Partner paid within 30 days of client receipt, **after receipt QC passes**. Rework time is not billable.
- Keep proof of partner's public liability / insurance evidence on file (renewed annually).

## 6. Records

Scope sheet + partner acceptance + first-article photo (if any) + receipt QC photos + scorecard entry + partner invoice → filed in `Drive: Partners/{Partner}/Jobs/{JobNo}/`.

---

**Rule:** a partner defect that reaches the client is our defect. The receipt QC exists so that never happens.
