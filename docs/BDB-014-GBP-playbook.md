# BDB-014 — Google Business Profile Playbook (Claim, Verify, Review Engine)

**Cost:** P0. **Time:** 2 days. **Why:** “embroidery Gaborone” local pack drives calls before SEO kicks in.

## 1. Claim & verify (Day 1)
1. business.google.com → Add business → **Branding & Decoration Botswana** → Category: `Embroidery shop` + `Promotional products supplier` + `Workwear store`
2. Location: workshop address (or “service area” if industrial lease pending). Hours: Mon–Fri 08–17, Sat 08–12. Phone: same as `WHATSAPP_NUMBER` in `store/assets/app.js`.
3. Verify: video verification (show signage, machines, stock). Keep utility bill + CIPA cert PDF on phone.

## 2. Optimize (Day 1)
- Description 750c: “Programme-grade branding partner — embroidery, DTF, screen, sublimation, PPE/ workwear supply, kitting, multi-site delivery, certificate pack. Gaborone, delivering to Jwaneng, Palapye, Francistown, Maun, Kasane & mine gates. Instant quotes at brandinganddecoration.co.bw”
- 10 photos (min): shop front, machine close-up (embroidery head), DTF press, first-article photo (ruler), packed cartons, delivery van, team, 2 client installs (with permission), workshop interior.
- Services: add each programme (workwear programme, PPE issue set, Client Brand Store, gifting retainer, event kit, tender pack).
- Products: import 5 from catalogue (golf shirt, conti, hi-vis vest, bottle, gazebo) with price bands, not single price.
- Website: `https://brandinganddecoration.co.bw` + appointment link → `/#builder`.

## 3. Posting cadence (2×/week, 5 min)
Mon: case study / programme note (1 photo + 100 words + CTA “Get a quote”). Thu: product/SEO page link. Repurpose store front: the SEO pages you publish (`store/seo/*.html`) become GBP posts.

## 4. Review engine (starts on first delivery — SOP-08)
- Request review on POD handover: WhatsApp with direct GBP review link + 1-sentence script: “If the delivery and quality were right, a Google review helps us win the next tender — 30 seconds?”
- Goal: 5 reviews in first 30 days (4.8+). Reply to every review within 24h (keyword: repeat “Branding & Decoration Botswana Gaborone”).
- Do not: fake reviews, buy reviews, keyword-stuff — ban.

## 5. Measure
Weekly: profile views, calls, direction requests, website clicks. Log in sheet alongside `BDB-012`. Target: +1 qualified inquiry per week from GBP by month 2.
