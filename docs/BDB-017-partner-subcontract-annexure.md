## 2. Scope (per job, written)
Principal issues written Scope Sheet per job (spec, qty × size, logo version + Pantone/thread, method, deadline, delivery, price). WhatsApp is coordination only. Partner acceptance in writing: “Accepted — {qty} × {product} by {deadline} at {price}.” No scope = no job.

## 3. Quality (annexure QC)
Partner follows SOP-03 (first-article), SOP-04/05 (production/QC), SOP-06 (receipt QC on our side). First-article photo required for >100 units or >P10k. Bulk sampling 1 in 20. Wash/cure tests where applicable. Defect target ≤2% rework, ≤3% partner defect.

## 4. Price + payment
Price per Scope Sheet, fixed for that job. Invoicing: Partner invoices Principal. Principal invoices client. **Partner paid within 30 days of client receipt, after receipt QC passes.** Rework is not billable. No price increase after acceptance.

## 5. Indemnity + insurance
Partner indemnifies Principal for defects caused by Partner work. Partner holds public liability (proof annually, min P1m). Partner warrants no counterfeit/untraceable safety goods.

## 6. Confidentiality + IP
Client logos, size matrices, pricing are confidential. Digitising files delivered are Principal property (source + DST). No sub-outsourcing without written consent.

## 7. Scorecard + 3-strike rule
Monthly: on-time %, defect %, communication (<4h), price adherence, capacity honesty. Published to Partner. 3 strikes in 6 months = removal. Top 2 get priority volume.

## 8. Term + termination
Month-to-month, either party 14d written. Immediate for safety, counterfeit, or confidentiality breach.

## 9. Governing law
Botswana law, Gaborone courts. Negotiation first.
